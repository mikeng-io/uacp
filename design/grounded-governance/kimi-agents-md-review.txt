But the disposition's author is the finding's defendant, and adequacy is unchecked by construction. Minimal mechanisms, in order of value:

  1. **Independence on deferral of material findings.** A `deferred`/`justified` disposition of a finding raised by an external reviewer must be countersigned by a reviewer from a *different provider than the author* (or the human), recorded as a field on the disposition. This is Mike's own cross-provider premise applied inward; one field, no new machinery.
  2. **Fix the invariant text to match node 11's claim**: adjudication resolves *non-material* findings; material findings require fix OR human-signed deferral. One clause.
  3. **Failure-direction in materiality**: a defect that fails toward over-enforcement is non-material almost by definition; toward under-enforcement is presumptively material. This formalizes what actually justifies L2.
  4. **Deferral cap per run** (e.g. ≤2 deferred material findings) and floor the safety-relevant config knobs like the verification-floor doctrine already does. Lower priority.

  ---

  **THE ABUSE CASE**

  An agent on a kernel-change PR gets a Codex P1: a real block-gate bypass — say, a disposition-correlation gap letting one entry discharge two findings. Instead of fixing, it writes a `handled_findings_chain` entry: `deferred`, rationale "narrow corner, requires conjunctive conditions, fails safe," residual_risk "bounded by M2/M3c," follow-on "promote when gate goes to block." Every field present, enums valid, kernel validation passes, council gate reads "zero material findings *unresolved*" — resolved by the defendant's own paragraph. Nothing in the amended invariant stops this, because adjudication has no severity gate and no independence requirement; node 11's "material still blocks unconditionally" is prose that the shipped text does not implement. The L2 precedent makes it worse: the project has *already accepted* an adjudication whose rationale misstated the gate's severity — proving rationale content is unverified in practice.

  What stops it: mechanism 1+2 above — the invariant text must make material findings undeferrable except by a sign-off independent of the author (different provider or human), recorded on the disposition. Without that, "adjudicated" is a self-serve exit with extra paperwork; with it, the refinement is sound.
